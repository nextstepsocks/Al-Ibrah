What's actually in a pair

No filler copy about "premium quality." Here's what we changed to make a sock worth keeping past one winter.

No ridge across the toes, on a foot four years old or forty. It's the first thing people notice when they stop noticing their socks.

Every batch is washed and dried forty times before it earns the AL IBRAH label — shape, color and elastic all have to hold.
