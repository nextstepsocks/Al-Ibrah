The Brand

Everyday essentials, made with care.

Al Ibrah was born from a simple idea: the socks you pull on each morning, the mat you stretch on, the bottle you carry through your day — deserve the same care as anything else you wear or use.

Based in the UAE, we design comfort-first socks and activewear accessories for men, women and kids, built for both city life and active life.

What started as a registered trademark in India has grown into a UAE-based lifestyle brand, trusted by families and athletes alike.
